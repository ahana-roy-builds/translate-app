# LocalLingo Privacy Policy

This repository hosts the public privacy policy for the offline-only LocalLingo
Private Beta. The published policy is available at:

<https://sivaramankh.github.io/locallingo-privacy/>

Non-confidential privacy questions may be submitted using the repository's
privacy issue form. Issues are public; do not include personal information,
conversation content, screenshots, crash logs, device identifiers, or other
sensitive information.
