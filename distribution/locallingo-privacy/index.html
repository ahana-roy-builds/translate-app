LocalLingo Private Beta

Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026

This policy applies to the offline-only LocalLingo Private Beta distributed through Apple TestFlight. LocalLingo is an iPhone conversation translator that uses on-device speech recognition, translation, and speech playback.

Conversation processing

LocalLingo processes microphone audio, recognized text, translations, and spoken translations on your iPhone. The app does not send conversation audio, transcripts, translations, or saved history to the developer or to a developer-operated server. Raw microphone audio is not saved.

This beta has no user accounts, advertising, analytics SDK, purchases, cloud synchronization, or online translation service. It does not track you or sell personal information.

Information stored on your iPhone

  • App preferences, including selected languages and speech settings.
  • Text-only conversation history, but only if you enable Save history on this iPhone. This setting is off by default.

Saved history is protected by iOS Data Protection, excluded from device backups, and retained until you delete it in LocalLingo or remove the app. You can delete all saved history from LocalLingo Settings. The app never stores microphone audio in conversation history.

Apple services and downloads

LocalLingo uses Apple system frameworks. Downloading or managing speech, translation, and voice assets can communicate with Apple even though conversation processing remains on-device. LocalLingo does not include your conversation content in those asset-download requests. Apple's handling of its services is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple also operates TestFlight. Apple may process beta distribution data, diagnostics, and feedback under its own terms. If you voluntarily submit TestFlight feedback, the developer may receive the report contents and related device or diagnostic details supplied by Apple. Do not include private conversation content in a feedback report.

Sharing

If you choose to share saved text using the iOS share sheet, the receiving app or service controls the shared copy under its own privacy practices. LocalLingo does not share it automatically.

Changes to this policy

A material change to the beta's data handling will be reflected here by updating the effective date before the changed build is distributed. A future LocalLingo build that offers online processing will require a revised policy and an in-app disclosure before any conversation is sent.

Contact

For a privacy question about this beta, open TestFlight, select LocalLingo, and choose Send Beta Feedback. Please do not include sensitive conversation content.

For a non-confidential question, you may instead use the public privacy question form. GitHub issues are public. Do not post names, contact details, conversation content, screenshots, crash logs, device identifiers, or other sensitive information.

This notice is limited to the offline-only LocalLingo Private Beta.
