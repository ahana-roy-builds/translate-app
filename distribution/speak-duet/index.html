A little help with your next conversation.

Speak Duet translates conversations on your iPhone. Prepare your languages before you need them, then keep talking with on-device translation.

Get started

  1. Complete setup and choose your default language and your conversation partner’s language.
  2. Connect to the internet and prepare the required language resources. You can also find these in Settings → Language Packs.
  3. Allow microphone and speech recognition access when requested. Wait for the selected pair to report ready.
  4. Start a conversation. Use one speaker at a time and pause briefly between phrases.

Requires an iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Language resources and availability depend on Apple’s services and your device.

Common questions

Can I translate without an internet connection?

Yes, after the required language resources are installed and translation access is valid. Prepare and verify the selected language pair while online before traveling. Signing in, downloading resources, checking subscription renewals, and restoring purchases need a connection.

How do I change my default language?

Open Settings → Default language. Your default language is saved separately from the pair currently selected for a conversation.

A language download is stalled or unavailable.

Check your connection and available device storage, then reopen Settings → Language Packs and check the pair’s Listen and Translate readiness. Apple manages these resources. An unavailable resource can prevent that pair from working on your device.

Do I need an account?

No. You can translate and subscribe as a guest. Optional Apple or Google sign-in creates a Speak Duet account; it does not upload or synchronize conversation history or language settings.

How do the free period and subscription work?

Completing setup starts 14 days of free translation on that device without authorizing billing. When that period ends, you can choose the monthly subscription. Eligible subscribers can receive an additional two-week Apple introductory trial. The App Store shows your offer and local price before you confirm. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled.

Purchases made in the TestFlight beta use Apple’s sandbox and do not charge you. Test subscription periods are accelerated.

How do I restore or cancel a subscription?

In Speak Duet Settings, choose Restore purchases or Manage subscription. Restore using the Apple Account that made the purchase. This can be different from the account used to sign in to Speak Duet. Signing out, deleting your Speak Duet account, or uninstalling the app does not cancel Apple billing.

You can also manage or cancel your subscription through Apple.

My subscription renewed, but translation is locked.

Connect to the internet, check that your iPhone’s date and time are correct, and choose Restore purchases in Settings. If Apple has not confirmed a new subscription period, reconnecting lets the app verify renewal. Existing saved text remains available when translation access ends.

How do I delete my account or saved conversations?

To delete your account, open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm with the same Apple or Google account. You can choose to delete local text history at the same time. To delete only history, use Settings → Delete all history. Account deletion does not cancel your Apple subscription or delete your Apple or Google account.

Contact us during the beta

Open TestFlight, select Speak Duet, and choose Send Beta Feedback for help, privacy questions, or account-deletion assistance.

For a problem report, include the iPhone model, iOS version, app version, selected languages, and the steps that led to the problem. Please leave out private conversations, passwords, payment details, and screenshots containing personal information.

Translations can contain mistakes. Check important details with the other person. Speak Duet is not an emergency service or a replacement for a qualified interpreter.
