Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy covers the Speak Duet iPhone app provided by Ahana Roy, including its TestFlight beta, optional accounts, and Apple subscriptions.

Conversation processing stays on your iPhone. Optional account services process identity information, and Apple manages subscription billing. Account services do not receive your conversation audio, translations, or saved history from Speak Duet.

Conversations and local storage

Speak Duet processes microphone audio, speech recognition, translation, and spoken playback on your iPhone. The app does not upload conversation content to a developer-operated translation service. Raw microphone audio is not saved by the app.

Language preferences and app settings are stored on your device. Text-only conversation history is off by default. If you enable it, saved text uses iOS Data Protection and is excluded from device backups. You can delete history in the app. Sharing text through the system share sheet sends the selected content to the app or recipient you choose, whose privacy practices then apply.

Downloading and managing Apple language resources requires communication with Apple. Speak Duet does not attach conversation content to those requests. Apple’s services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Optional accounts and service providers

You can use Speak Duet and purchase a subscription without creating a Speak Duet account. If you choose Apple or Google sign-in, the selected provider and Firebase Authentication process information needed to identify and authenticate you. This can include your name, email address or Apple relay email address, account identifier, sign-in credentials or tokens, and technical information such as IP address and user agent. The app does not ask you to create a separate password.

Google’s sign-in service may also process profile information, including an associated phone number, device identifiers, approximate location, usage information, and other service data for functionality and analytics. Firebase Authentication declares diagnostic collection for analytics. These provider activities are separate from on-device conversation processing. Speak Duet does not request your contacts or GPS location.

Firebase retains account information until deletion is requested. Provider security logs and backup copies can remain for the periods described in the provider’s policies. Google states that Firebase Authentication processes data in the United States. See Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s privacy policy.

Speak Duet does not include advertising or use account information to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. We have not enabled a separate Google Analytics, advertising, or Crashlytics service in the app. Authentication providers still handle their own operational and diagnostic data.

Free access and subscriptions

The app keeps a device-only Keychain record of the start and expiry of initial free access and the most recently observed date. It is not synchronized to your Speak Duet account. Keychain data can remain after uninstalling the app, so reinstalling is not a guaranteed way to reset free access.

Apple processes purchases, billing, refunds, and subscription management. The app reads StoreKit purchase and entitlement information to determine translation access. Speak Duet does not receive your payment card or bank details and does not upload purchase evidence to a developer-operated server in this version. Your Apple purchasing account can differ from your optional Speak Duet account.

Deletion and your choices

  • Account: In Settings → Account, choose Delete account and confirm with the same sign-in provider. This deletes your Firebase account identity; Apple sign-in authorization is revoked when applicable. It does not delete your Apple or Google account.
  • History: Use Settings → Delete all history, or select the option to erase local history during account deletion. Account deletion alone does not erase it unless you choose that option.
  • Subscription: Cancel separately through Apple. Deleting an account or uninstalling Speak Duet does not cancel billing.
  • Permissions: You can change microphone and speech-recognition permissions in iPhone Settings. Voice features need these permissions.

Account deletion does not erase Apple’s purchase records, copies you have shared with other apps, or provider records retained under their own policies. Contact us if you need help making a privacy request or cannot use in-app deletion.

Feedback, website, and contact

When you send beta feedback, Apple may share your message, attachments, device details, and diagnostics with the developer. Only include information you want us to review. We use feedback to provide support and investigate app problems.

These support pages use no advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts, or cookies set by our page code. When served through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement. Opening a link to another service is subject to that service’s privacy practices.

During the beta, open TestFlight, select Speak Duet, and choose Send Beta Feedback to contact us about privacy, support, or account deletion. Please do not include private conversations, passwords, or payment details.

We will update this policy when the app’s data handling changes and revise the date above. See the support page for help using Speak Duet.
